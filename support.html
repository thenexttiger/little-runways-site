How to play

  • Draw a path: touch a plane and drag. End your line near a glowing runway approach and the plane lines itself up and lands.
  • Hold: double-tap a plane to make it circle.
  • Departures: drag a waiting plane onto its runway, then draw it to the matching exit on the edge of the map.
  • Altitude: a plane's shadow sits further away the higher it flies. Planes at different heights can cross safely.
  • Safety lights: if two planes get too close they break away and you lose a light. Lose all three and the shift ends.

Common questions

Why did my plane go around? The runway was still busy with the previous landing or a departure. Space your arrivals a little further apart.

Why are iPhone and iPad scores ranked separately? The iPad shows more sky, so it gets its own leaderboards to keep competition fair.
