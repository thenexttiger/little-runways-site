You're the controller

Real ATC calls, one tap away.

Tap any plane to take control. Everything a controller would do, without the jargon.

  • Climb to 5,000 or 7,000 ft to pass safely over crossing traffic
  • Slow down or speed up to space out the queue
  • Switch runways, or send a plane into a holding pattern
  • Release departures into the gaps between arrivals

When things go wrong

Fires, closures and Maydays.

Now and then a landing goes wrong, a runway catches fire and the crews close it. Works shut runways for repairs. Planes on approach drop into holds, and it's up to you to re-route everyone to the runway that's still open.

  • Mayday emergencies head straight in and need a clear runway, now
  • Medical flights count double if you land them on time
  • Rush hours arrive in waves, so plan for the next one

Weather and terrain

Fly around storms. Respect the mountains.

Storm cells drift across the map and planes must steer clear of them. Mountain peaks are no-fly zones, so approaches thread through valleys. And when the wind shifts, every plane has to land the other way.

The heart of it

Calm on the surface. Clever underneath.

Lines can cross. The game predicts where every plane will be and only warns you about real conflicts, so you plan ahead instead of panicking. Every shift runs from sunrise to runway lights.

Nine hand-made airports

Crossing runways, a one-way mountain strip, two airports sharing one sky, a three-runway hub. Learn each map and your plans change.
