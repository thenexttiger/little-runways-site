Little Runways

Privacy policy · Last updated 21 October 2026

Little Runways is a game. It does not collect, store or share personal data, and it contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stars, best scores and settings) is saved on your device. If you use iCloud, a small copy of that progress syncs between your own devices through Apple's iCloud key-value storage. We never see it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores are submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards under your Game Center profile. Apple's privacy policy covers that service. We do not receive your Apple Account details.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone.
